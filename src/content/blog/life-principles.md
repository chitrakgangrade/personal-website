*Written on one particular day -- a snapshot of my thinking then, not a permanent contract with myself. If future-me disagrees with any of this, future-me wins.*

## What are my life principles?

- **Be excited most days.** I want to wake up looking forward to something -- doesn't have to be big, just genuine. If that stops being true for a stretch, it's a signal to change something, not a rule I have to hit every single morning.
- **Be wealthy enough that money stops dictating my choices.** Not a specific number -- a threshold. Past it, decisions should be about what I want, not just what I can afford.
- **Stay frugal, but comfortable.** Don't spend more than I need to. Partly practical, partly the same reason as above -- not letting money, or the spending of it, have power over my choices.
- **Be as fit as I can be.**
- **Stay intellectually stimulated.** Read more, learn more, talk to interesting people, get a little better at thinking every day.
